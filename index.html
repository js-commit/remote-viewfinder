Install help

Phone

  1. Open the APK on your phone and allow your browser to install apps.
  2. For tap to record, tap Enable next to "Tap control" in the app. If Android says it's restricted, go to App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.
  3. For the watch shutter in Samsung Camera, set Volume key function → Take picture or record video.

Watch

  1. Turn on Developer options, ADB debugging and Wireless debugging on the watch.
  2. From a computer on the same Wi‑Fi: 
    adb pair <watch-ip>:<pairing-port>
adb connect <watch-ip>:<port>
adb install remote-viewfinder-watch.apk
  3. Phone and watch apps must come from the same release.

Questions

Why does Android ask to share my screen every time?

Android requires it for every session, and no app can skip it. That's what keeps anything from quietly watching your screen.

Does it work with my camera app?

Yes. It mirrors whatever camera app you open. Built and tested on a Galaxy Z Fold with Samsung Camera.

Is it private?

No account, no cloud, no analytics. The viewer is served by your phone on your own Wi‑Fi, behind a private link. Treat that link like a password, since it can tap your phone.

The viewer won't load.

Put both devices on the same Wi‑Fi and open the exact link or QR code the phone shows.

The watch shutter stopped responding.

A known issue on some watches. Turn the watch's Bluetooth off and on to restore it. A fix is on the way.
